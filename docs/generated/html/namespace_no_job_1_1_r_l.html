inline

Use obstacle DQN in its core and learned axis tracking on the open exterior.

The controller switch and boundary projection are explicit engineering rules, not an end-to-end learned hierarchy. Neither actor uses NavMesh or a route. The exterior is obstacle-free in the validated arena. At the core boundary, the tracking actor continues toward the true goal instead of treating the proxy as arrival.

Refine with a balanced mix of random episodes and the learner's own failures.

One in four episodes revisits a failed pair. Failures are refreshed every 500 episodes; actions still use epsilon-greedy DQN and the original rewards only.

Returns
Refined weights, or no result when cancelled. No graph/path teacher is used.
